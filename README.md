## 6. How to Contribute

These lists get better when people who actually sat the interview say what was asked. Corrections, sharper wording and genuinely new questions are all welcome.

### Pick a template

| Submitting | Use | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| An interview question and its answer | [`Template_QnA.md`](templates/Template_QnA.md) | The common case. |
| A concept explainer, not a question | [`Template_Theory.md`](templates/Template_Theory.md) | Notes that teach an idea. |
| A problem with runnable code and tests | [`Template_Coding.md`](templates/Template_Coding.md) | In-browser coding problems. |

Every field in those templates maps one-to-one onto our question ingestion contract. That is deliberate: when a submission is approved, the fields load straight into our ingestion form and the question reaches the live bank without anyone re-typing it. **Please don't rename keys or leave required fields blank** — a submission that doesn't parse can't be loaded automatically and takes considerably longer to land.

### The quality bar

A submission is accepted when it is:

- **Real.** Asked in an actual interview, or a concept those interviews genuinely rely on. Not invented to pad a list.
- **Not already covered.** Search the lists and the bank first. A rephrasing of an existing question is a duplicate.
- **Discriminating.** A good question separates someone who understands the idea from someone who has memorised the definition.
- **Answerable in the stated time.** If your own answer runs past `time_minutes`, either the estimate or the scope is wrong.
- **Sourced.** Say where it came from. Submissions with no stated source are declined — we can't verify them and we won't publish what we can't stand behind.
- **Yours to give.** Don't paste copyrighted material from books, paid courses or another site.

The bar rises as the lists fill up. Once an area is well covered, a new question usually has to *replace* one rather than join it — say which, and why yours is better.

### How your PR gets reviewed

Every submission is read by someone on the 123ofAI team — the same people who maintain the question bank. We check the question against the bar above, verify the technical content, and edit for clarity where it helps. Expect comments rather than a silent close: if we ask for changes, it is because we intend to merge it once they land.

Outcomes are one of three: **merged**, **changes requested**, or **declined with a reason**. We don't close submissions without saying why.

### How long it takes

Review is manual and done by a small team alongside the rest of their work, so **please be patient** — a considered review of a technical question takes longer than a rubber stamp, and that is the point.

If something genuinely needs to move faster — a factual error on a live question, a hiring deadline — email **contact@aspirefrontiers.com** with the PR link and we will pull it forward.

### A few practical things

- **One question per pull request.** Batched submissions get reviewed at the speed of their weakest entry.
- **Never edit `README.md`.** It is generated from `data/`. Run `python3 scripts/generate_readme.py` after changing data, and commit the result.
- **Corrections don't need a template.** A typo or a wrong claim can just be a PR with the fix and a one-line explanation.
- **Not sure it qualifies?** Open an issue and ask before writing it up.
- **Anonymity is fine.** `company_tag` can be left blank; we will never publish an employer you didn't name yourself.
