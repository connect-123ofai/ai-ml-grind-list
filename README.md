## 6. How to Contribute

> These lists get better when the people who actually sat the interview say what was asked.

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/PRs-welcome-EC4899?style=flat-square"> <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Review-by%20the%20123ofAI%20team-64748B?style=flat-square">

### Pick a template

| Submitting | Template | For |
|:--|:--|:--|
| An interview question and its answer | [`Template_QnA.md`](templates/Template_QnA.md) | The common case |
| A concept explainer, not a question | [`Template_Theory.md`](templates/Template_Theory.md) | Notes that teach an idea |
| A problem with runnable code and tests | [`Template_Coding.md`](templates/Template_Coding.md) | In-browser coding problems |

**Not sure what a good submission looks like?** Two worked examples, filled in end to end:

- [`Example_QnA.md`](templates/Example_QnA.md) — a Medium classical-ML question, answered
- [`Example_Coding.md`](templates/Example_Coding.md) — a NumPy attention problem with a solution and tests that pass

Every field maps one-to-one onto our question ingestion contract. That's deliberate: an approved submission loads straight into our ingestion form and reaches the live bank without anyone re-typing it. **Don't rename keys or leave required fields blank** — a submission that doesn't parse can't be loaded automatically and takes considerably longer to land.

### The quality bar

| | |
|:--|:--|
| **Real** | Asked in an actual interview, or a concept those interviews genuinely rely on. Not invented to pad a list. |
| **Not already covered** | Search the lists and the bank first. A rephrasing of an existing question is a duplicate. |
| **Discriminating** | It should separate someone who understands the idea from someone who memorised the definition. |
| **Answerable in the stated time** | If your own answer runs past `time_minutes`, the estimate or the scope is wrong. |
| **Sourced** | Say where it came from. No stated source is declined — we won't publish what we can't stand behind. |
| **Yours to give** | No copyrighted material from books, paid courses or another site. |

The bar rises as the lists fill up. Once an area is well covered, a new question usually has to *replace* one rather than join it — say which, and why yours is better.

### How your PR gets reviewed

Every submission is read by someone on the 123ofAI team — the same people who maintain the question bank. We check it against the bar above, verify the technical content, and edit for clarity where that helps. Expect comments rather than a silent close: if we ask for changes, it's because we intend to merge it once they land.

Outcomes are one of three — **merged**, **changes requested**, or **declined with a reason**. We don't close submissions without saying why.

### How long it takes

Review is manual, done by a small team alongside the rest of their work. **Please be patient** — a considered review of a technical question takes longer than a rubber stamp, and that's the point.

If something genuinely needs to move faster — a factual error on a live question, a hiring deadline — email **contact@aspirefrontiers.com** with the PR link and we'll pull it forward.

<details>
<summary><b>A few practical things</b></summary>

- **One question per pull request.** Batched submissions get reviewed at the speed of their weakest entry.
- **Never edit `README.md`.** It's generated from `data/`. Run `python3 scripts/generate_readme.py` and commit the result.
- **Corrections don't need a template.** A typo or wrong claim can just be a PR with the fix and a one-line explanation.
- **Not sure it qualifies?** Open an issue and ask before writing it up.
- **Anonymity is fine.** `company_tag` can be blank; we'll never publish an employer you didn't name yourself.

</details>
